# Coral Key response-gate measurement

Does the correctness-keyed response gate (`SimulationConfig.reproduction_correctness_weight`,
TattleTots lever 5) move either falsification clause on the ReefWatch fishery/IUU
instrument, at fixed initial parameters?

The thing under test is the **ordinary (evolved)** arm. The designed and oracle arms are
a ceiling reference, not the subject: they are hand-written policies, so their precision
says what the instrument affords, not what selection found. Under the same `W=1` config the
designed reporter reaches 58.69% precision (all-designed) and 56.92% (invasion) against the
12.80% null, so the exploitable margin here is large and the evolved arm's ~19.6% is not a
ceiling artifact.

## Verdict

- **Clause 1 (correct-report rate rises over generations within a run): moved, and
  positive on this instrument, but not attributable to the gate alone.** With levers 1-4
  on and the gate off (`W=0`) the rate already rises in 13/20 primary and 18/20 holdout
  seeds. Turning the gate on (`W=1`) roughly doubles the mean slope
  (+0.0020 → +0.0045 primary, +0.0037 → +0.0066 holdout), lifts the realized adult
  correct-report rate from ~17.7% to ~19.6% against a **12.80%** static-prior null, and
  raises the rising-seed count to 15/20 and 19/20. The direction replicates in an
  independent seed block and the paired per-seed slope delta is positive in 12/20 and
  16/20 seeds. This is the same shape as SparseSensor (gate ≈ 2-3× the reserves-only
  slope), at a smaller relative margin over the null.
- **Clause 2 (parent-child reproductive correlation reliably above ~0.2): not met.**
  Mean parent-child offspring correlation is −0.083 (primary) and −0.060 (holdout) at
  `W=0` and +0.006 / +0.005 at `W=1`; 0/20 and 1/20 seeds exceed 0.2. The gate moves the
  correlation up by ~+0.07 on average, from negative to approximately zero, and nowhere
  near the threshold. The mechanism is the same one that limited SparseSensor, and it is
  worse here: ~94% of adult agent-steps are already eligible to reproduce while the
  population cap binds on ~91-95% of steps, so merit ordering only decides *who goes
  first* in an almost-always-saturated queue, and per-lineage output stays near
  replacement (mean offspring 1.31 for ever-correct adults versus 1.10 for
  reporting-but-never-correct adults).

## Reproduction

Locked dependency: `tattletots @ 5c1534a6b1dd5fe4f5ddabce54b8b3733af1cf6c` in `uv.lock`.

```bash
uv sync --locked --no-build --no-binary-package coral-key --no-binary-package domain-runner \
  --no-binary-package tattletots --extra dev

# ordinary (evolved) arm, W=0 control and W=1 treatment, primary seed block
```

Artifacts: `docs/response_gate_primary/`, `docs/response_gate_holdout/`,
`docs/response_gate_designed/`. The per-arm Markdown report in each directory is

## What was measured versus inferred

Measured directly, per run, from `tattletots.telemetry.payoff_ledger.PayoffLedger`:
realized adult correct-report rate, the per-generation regression slope of the
correct-report rate, parent-child offspring correlation, reports per adult lifetime,
silent-adult share, eligible-to-reproduce share, cap-binding step share, and per-group
mean offspring. The static-prior and uniform nulls come from the harness null
measurement at the same seeds and window.

Inferred, not measured: the *causal* attribution of the clause-1 improvement to the gate
rather than to a correlated side effect of the gate. `W=1` reduces reports per adult
lifetime (~95 → ~86 primary) and reduces cap binding slightly, so part of the precision
gain is plausibly a reporting-volume effect (fewer, better-selected reports) rather than
a lineage-level competence gain. The paired per-seed deltas are the evidence that the
direction is not a seed artifact; they are not evidence about the pathway.

## Artifacts and caveats

- The static-prior null here is **12.80%** at a 600-step window, not the 14.84% quoted in
  [`designed_reporter_measurement.md`](designed_reporter_measurement.md), which was
  measured at 200 steps. The null is window-dependent; every number in this document is
  read against the 600-step value, and the 14.84% figure should not be carried over to
  600-step runs.
- Generations observed per run *fall* under `W=1` (18.1 → 12.8 primary, 22.9 → 14.1
  holdout), so the clause-1 slope is fitted over fewer generation points in the treatment
  arm. A slope per generation is not directly a slope per step; the treatment arm has
  longer-lived, slower-turnover lineages.
- The parent-child *precision* correlation is above 0.2 in the `W=0` arm (+0.250 primary,
  +0.228 holdout) and lower under `W=1`. That quantity is not clause 2 — clause 2 is
  about reproductive output — and it is computed only over parent-child pairs where both
  issued at least one report, so it is a much smaller and more selected sample. It should
  not be read as a clause-2 clearance.
- Clause-2 correlations in individual seeds range from −0.16 to +0.21; the single holdout
  seed above 0.2 is at the edge of that spread and is not treated as a clearance.
- NumPy emits a handful of `overflow encountered in square` / `in reduce` warnings per
  20-seed block, in both the `W=0` and `W=1` arms. They arise inside a `float64`
  variance/standard-deviation reduction. The only such reductions on unbounded data are
  engine-side (`np.var` on stream and residual vectors in `tattletots.models.stream` and
  `tattletots.engine.residual`, whose magnitudes can grow across 600 steps) — that
  localization is inference, not something traced to a specific run: the warnings are
  seed-dependent and did not reproduce under `-W error::RuntimeWarning` on seeds 42-45.
  They do not touch the numbers reported here: every clause and reporting-economics
  metric is computed from integer counts held in the payoff ledger (reports, correct
  reports, offspring, generation indices), and no `NaN` or `Infinity` appears in any
  emitted artifact.
